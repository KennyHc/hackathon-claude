Phases & drift

Which data do we get, and when does it change?

Four years of TRAIN with answers, then six months of LIVE for the leaderboard, then FINAL at 18:00 for one scored shot. The month-by-month volume is steady, about 31,000 lines a month. What changes is how the data looks.

How many bank lines arrive each month?

Bank lines per booking month, TRAIN and LIVE counted from bank_transactions. FINAL is drawn at its announced size (about 220,000 lines over 6 months). Numbered markers are the known drift events.

    The files

    How do the files connect?

    A bank line on one side, open items on the other. Most lines link directly, through the payer name and the amount. The rest link through an evidence file that lists what one line contains: a batch, a payment run, a remittance advice. Counts below are for LIVE.

    Where the points are

    Which kinds of cases hold the points?

    How are the 100 points split across families of cases?

    ≈ points = 30 × share of bank lines + 50 × share of (line, item) pairs, in a LIVE-like window (TRAIN, Jan–Jun 2025). That is what a family would earn if it were solved perfectly. The 20 exception points are scored per code, shown in the next section.

    Are the lines and the pairs made of the same cases?

    No. Each band is one family. Top: its share of bank lines (30 points). Bottom: its share of pairs (50 points). Remittances and batches are thin on top and wide below: few lines, hundreds of items each.

    Which individual case types matter most?

    The segment column of the golden, ranked by ≈ points. Faded bar = share of lines, solid bar = share of pairs, both on the same 0–22 % scale. Hover a row for the counts.

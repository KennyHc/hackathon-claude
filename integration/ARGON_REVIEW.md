### 1. Bugs & Wrong Assumptions in [`integration/PLAN.md`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/PLAN.md) (Ranked by Score Impact)

* **Stage-order trimming vs. global chronological [`Ledger.apply`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/ledger.py#L228-L327) (~5–10 pts):** Walking balances stage-by-stage (`batch` → `refs` → `party`) conflicts with [`Ledger.apply`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/ledger.py#L228), which sorts globally by `(booking_date, bank_txn_id)`. If Stage 1 clears an item on Apr 1 and Stage 2 matches an earlier payment on Feb 1, `open_at(Feb 1)` permits Stage 2, causing over-allocation on Apr 1 (and a false `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` in [`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L147)). Worse, trimming `amount := open` ([`PLAN.md`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/PLAN.md#L17-L19)) leaves broken 1-to-$N$ lines claimed (blocking later stages) and sets `open_after = 0`. **Fix:** Drop the *entire bank-line claim* in stage $k$ if it over-allocates any item across the phase, and never silently trim `amount := open` unless the stage flags `PARTIAL`.
* **[`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py) misbehavior on real allocations (~6–10 pts across 5 codes):**
  * **`OVERPAYMENT` ([`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L291-L297)):** Fires on any single-row line with `open_after <= tol` and `cash > amount + 0.01`. Cascade trimming (`amount := open` makes `open_after = 0`, **not** `PARTIAL` as [`PLAN.md`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/PLAN.md#L11) assumes) or matching 1 of $N$ invoices triggers massive `OVERPAYMENT` FPs. Conversely, if a matcher outputs `amount = cash` within `0.5%` tol, true overpayments are missed.
  * **`PARTIAL` & `FX_DIFFERENCE` ([`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L274-L287)):** `PARTIAL` is not in [`MATCHING_PASSTHROUGH`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L34). If a matcher outputs `amount = cash` on an explained shortfall (`SHORT_*`) or FX spread (EM spreads reach 1.5% > 0.5% [`_tol`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L79)), `open_after > tol` forces `PARTIAL` *before* `FX_DIFFERENCE`/`SHORT_*` run. Conversely, on FX lines, if a matcher sets `amount = open_before` on a true partial payment, `open_after == 0` falsely assigns `FX_DIFFERENCE` without checking `cash`.
  * **`DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` ([`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L134-L154), [`ledger.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/ledger.py#L83-L194)):** Dropping `open <= 0.01` in the cascade prevents [`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L147) from ever detecting in-phase duplicates unless pre-tagged. Also, [`load_items`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/ledger.py#L75) omits items closed *before* phase start, blinding matchers to prior-phase duplicates/returns.
  * **`SHORT_BANK_CHARGES` ([`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L390-L404)):** Calls [`fx.convert`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/fx.py#L132) without `masters_dir`, crashing on `ZAR` and ignoring 2026 FX rates.
* **[`integration/run.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/run.py#L86) leaks matcher exceptions (~2–4 pts):** [`assign_exceptions`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L83) returns `None` for *no exception* (`""`), but `exc.where(exc.notna(), items.get("exception"))` falls back to B's guess whenever `exc` is `None`.

1. **`pr.py` `(entity, document_number, |document_amount|)` ignores supplier & payment factory**: Supplier `document_number`s (`2024/00150`, `1350`—132k unique across 1.53M AP rows in [EDA.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/EDA.md#L505)) collide within an entity, and `ES00-PENA-EUR` pays for `{ES00, ES01, ES02, PT01}` ([ES01_202501_0.csv](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/data/val/payment_runs/ES01_202501_0.csv#L2)). **Fix**: Take `entity` from filename (`<entity>_<YYYYMM>_<n>.csv`), match `(entity, supplier_id, document_number, |document_amount|)` via `beneficiary_iban`/`beneficiary`, dropping `supplier_id` only for factors (`ap_factored`).

Dropping the whole batch on one miss kills recall. **Fix**: Resolve `customer_id` via `mandate_id`/`debtor_iban`, match suffix + `amount`, and emit resolved items partially.
3. **`cards.py` `order_number → document_number` & exact date**: Web orders (`WEB24-...`, `TW21-...`) live in `po_number` (`customer_po` in [`load_items`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/ledger.py#L45)), not `document_number`, and `booking_date` lags `settlement_date` by 0–5d. **Fix**: Index AR by `po_number`; link bank lines by `settlement_id` or `(account_id, merchant_id, Σnet)` within `settlement_date + [0..5]d`.
4. **`cheques.py` blank/prefixless `stub_reference`**: Blank on 14.8% of rows ([EDA.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/EDA.md#L710)) or prefixless (`231325` vs `FA240231325` in [FR01_LYO_202501.csv](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/data/val/cheque_deposits/FR01_LYO_202501.csv#L12-L15)). **Fix**: Resolve `drawer` → `customer_id`, match `stub_reference` suffix, and fall back to exact `amount` (FIFO) on that drawer's open AR.
5. **`remit.py` `payment_ref` in bank text**: Bank lines often omit `payment_ref` or book before `received_date` ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L126-L130)). **Fix**: Fallback link by `(entity, customer_id, |bank_amount - advice_total| <= 0.01, |booking_date - payment_date| <= 7d)`, matching `your_order` (`po_number`) and invoice suffixes.

### 3. Amount Convention Traps
- **FX**: Emit `amount` in **item currency** (`open_amount`), never `bank.amount`. Match FX transfers via `orig_amount`, `/OCMT/`, or [`fx.convert`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/fx.py#L46) (±3%). Never tag credit notes (`APC`/`ARC`) with `FX_DIFFERENCE`.
- **Netted Credit Notes**: `document_amount` in `payment_runs/` is **negative**; sum signed amounts for `transfer_amount`, but emit **positive** `abs(document_amount)`, `exception=""`.
- **`SHORT_DISPUTE` vs `SHORT_DISCOUNT`**: Dispute `reason` (`DAMAGED`, `PRICE DIFF`, etc.) → `amount = paid`, `exception = "SHORT_DISPUTE"`; early-payment discount → `amount = open_amount` so [`exceptions.py`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/classification/exceptions.py#L120-L143) sets `SHORT_DISCOUNT`.
- **`RETURNED`**: Emit positive reversed `amount`, `exception = "RETURNED"`; [`Ledger.allocate`](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/integration/lib/ledger.py#L164) re-opens the item so `re_presentation=Y` rows avoid false `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT`.
- **Confirming per maturity date**: Group by **transfer** (`end_to_end_id`) with maturity = `max(due_date)` of its invoices (CNs have `due_date = issue_date`), checking `count(transfers) == N PROVEEDORES` and `Σ transfer_amount == |bank.amount|` ([ES02-RIBA-EUR_202501.n43](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/data/val/bank_statements/ES02-RIBA-EUR/ES02-RIBA-EUR_202501.n43#L8-L52)).
- **Aggregated runs**: Most `run_type == "aggregated"` rows settle per `end_to_end_id`; allocate whole `run_id` only when text has `FICHERO C34 PR...` and `|bank.amount| == Σ run transfer_amount`.
- **Card gross vs net**: Bank line = `Σ net`, but allocations emit `amount = gross`, `exception = ""`.

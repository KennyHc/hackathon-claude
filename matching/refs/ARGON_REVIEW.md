### (1) 3 Biggest Precision Risks & Gates
1. **Truncated multi-ref partial emission ([PLAN.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/matching/refs/PLAN.md#L40)):** Emitting only visible items when `cash > sum(cited)` guarantees 0% txn accuracy on that line and blocks K3 from claiming it ([PLAN_LUIGI.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/PLAN_LUIGI.md#L30)). **Gate:** Delete this rule; either resolve `cash − sum(cited)` against same-party open items or leave the line to K3.
2. **Unverified short/digit-only/PO/fuzzy hits ([PLAN.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/matching/refs/PLAN.md#L21-L30)):** 4-char tokens, stripped prefixes, PO numbers, and entity-wide 1-edit fuzzy hits collide with `ar_ref_noise` (12.7 pts in K3) and `UNIDENTIFIED` lookalikes ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L52)). **Gate:** Filter by `line_labels.p_items`; allow non-exact/PO/digit-only/fuzzy hits only when counterparty (name/IBAN/group) matches the item's party.
3. **Single-item `OVERPAYMENT` (`cash > open`, [PLAN.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/matching/refs/PLAN.md#L38)):** Any 1-to-N line with an unextracted second ref has `cash > open` and gets falsely claimed as `OVERPAYMENT` (~0.1 pts). **Gate:** Drop `OVERPAYMENT` from K2 (defer to K3), or require matched party, no multi-ref/truncation cues, and no other open items for that party.

### (2) Amount-Resolution Ordering & Ambiguities
- **Wrong matching tolerance ([PLAN.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/matching/refs/PLAN.md#L35)):** Using the scorer's `0.5%` band (`max(0.01, 0.5%)`) for rule matching makes `full sum` swallow `<0.5%` fees/disputes and blurs point rules (`1.25%/1.5%` withholding vs `1–2%` discount vs `≤2%` bank charges). Use **cent tolerance (`±0.02`)** for same-currency point rules.
- **Late-discount trap:** A discount taken *after* `issue + days + 3` is `PARTIAL` (`amount = cash`, [POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L44-L45)). In [PLAN.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/matching/refs/PLAN.md#L36-L37), it fails step 3 and falsely matches step 5 (`bank charges ≤ 2%`, emitting full `open_amount`).
- **Correct order:** Split `line.currency == item.currency` vs `FX_DIFFERENCE` (`orig_amount` exact, else reference rate `≤3%`) first. Same-currency order: (1) `full sum` (`±0.02`), (2) `+ open CNs` (`related_document`, then same-party CNs), (3) `discount within terms` (`±0.02`), (4) **late discount** (`PARTIAL`, `amount = cash`), (5) `withholding` (`1.25/1.5/10%` of `total`, gated to MX/BR/MA customers), (6) exact fractions (`instalment`, `retention`, `advance`), (7) `bank charges` (non-SEPA cross-border only, `≤60 EUR` and `≤2%`), (8) keyword-gated `PARTIAL`.

### (4) [POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md) Rules the Plan Gets Wrong
- **Amount convention ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L26-L35)):** Discount/withholding % apply to invoice **`total`**, while full-clearing shortfall rows emit **`open_amount`**. Initial `retention` and `advance`-deducted payments are `PARTIAL` and must emit **`amount = cash`**, not `open_amount`.
- **Credit notes ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L53)):** Rappel CNs have no `related_document`—match open CNs by **party** too ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L105-L107)). Under `FX_DIFFERENCE`, credit-note rows must keep `exception = ""`.
- **Instalments & Retention ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L43-L44)):** The **last** instalment and **retention release** match remaining `open_amount` (`cash == open_amount`, `amount = open_amount`, `exception = ""`), whereas prior instalments and initial retention deductions get `amount = cash`, `exception = PARTIAL`.
- **Replaced invoices ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L98-L100)):** When Y replaces X, purge **both X and its cancelling CN** from open items so neither is ever allocated.
- **Supplier refunds ([POLICIES.md](file:///Users/luigizheng/Desktop/Close_the_tabs/docs/POLICIES.md#L72-L73)):** A receipt citing `"REEMBOLSO FRA X"` settles the **`APC` item** (`related_document == X`), never `API` X. If no open `APC` exists, it is a duplicate-payment refund (`category = SUPPLIER_REFUND`, no item)—leave it unclaimed.

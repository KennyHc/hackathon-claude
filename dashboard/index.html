    Definitions and what's left out
    Score
    100 × (0.5 · allocation F1 + 0.3 · transaction accuracy + 0.2 · exception macro-F1). An amount counts as right within max(0.01, 0.5 %).
    Allocation precision / recall
    Share of the (bank line, item, amount) pairs we submit that are right / share of the true pairs we found.
    Transaction accuracy
    Share of bank lines where every row is right.
    Validation
    TRAIN Jan–Jun 2025 rebuilt to look like LIVE. Rules and thresholds were written while looking at it.
    Holdout
    TRAIN Jan–Jun 2024, built the same way and never used for tuning. Learned parts only see data before 2024-01-01.
    Max points
    What a segment adds going from 0 % to 100 %: 30 × its share of lines + 50 × its share of item pairs.
    Left out
    FINAL is scored only at the reveal. LIVE per-exception scores are not published, so the exception chart compares validation and holdout only. LIVE does not report every segment.
